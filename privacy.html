Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Uploader is a personal automation tool used solely by its own account owner to schedule and publish that owner's own videos to their own connected social media accounts (including TikTok).

Uploader accesses only the minimum data required to publish content on the account owner's behalf, via each platform's official API and OAuth authorization. Uploader does not collect, sell, or share any user data with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to publish content the account owner has personally selected.

The account owner may revoke Uploader's access at any time through the relevant platform's account settings.

Questions: contact the account owner directly.
